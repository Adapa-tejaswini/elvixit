Artificial intelligence is a field of computer science.
Machine learning allows computers to learn from data.
Deep learning uses neural networks to solve complex problems.
Natural language processing allows computers to understand human language.
Large language models learn patterns from large amounts of text.
Transformers are neural networks designed to process sequences.
Attention allows a model to understand relationships between words.
Artificial intelligence is changing the way people use computers.
Machine learning is used in healthcare, finance, education, and technology.
